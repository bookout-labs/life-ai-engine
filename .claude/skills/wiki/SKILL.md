description: Wiki procedures for the personal operating system.
---

Discover pages with pages.ts list and search. Read relevant pages before editing. Use pages.ts edit and save for existing prose, or write a body from stdin. Record source provenance with files.ts and link source pages by slug. Keep facts in Neon and originals in R2. Resolve links and preserve attribution. Use pages.ts delete for reversible removal; hard deletion is a migration.
