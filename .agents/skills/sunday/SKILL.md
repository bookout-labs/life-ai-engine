---
name: sunday
description: Sunday procedures for the personal operating system.
---

Run `node .agents/scripts/weekly.ts` for preparation data from Neon. It defaults to the next Monday, including today when today is Monday. Pass a start date as YYYY-MM-DD to reproduce another week. The JSON includes a seven-day schedule and bills, fourteen days of deadlines plus overdue items, members, relationships, active activities, and loan balances as of the start date. `overloadWeeks` covers the start week and the two after it. Show each overloaded week, its booked evening count, and its booked dates. `upkeep` lists home and car upkeep that is overdue, due in the week, or has no record; show it with its next due date. Undated deadlines and obligations without a billing day have separate lists. Empty lists mean no matching records. Null amounts or balances mean unknown. Unavailable summaries are listed explicitly. The command reads records in one read-only snapshot.

Call the Life AI MCP tool `finance_records` for accounts and recurring monthly budget targets from `packages/core/finances.ts`. Use its `budgetTargets` records for category targets in USD. `monthly_min` and `monthly_max` preserve ranges as decimal strings. Equal bounds mean an exact target. Keep `draft` proposals separate from `approved` limits and show `is_estimate` when true. An empty target list means targets have not been loaded. If the tool is unavailable, report that source as unavailable. Never reconstruct targets from the finances page or infer spending actuals from targets.

Review active goals by area through the Life AI MCP tool `goals` and follow each plan page for progress; report an unavailable tool explicitly. When a goal is met or abandoned, mark it with `set_goal_status`.

Read current weekly and areas pages through pages.ts for household check-in preferences, routine, decisions and questions. Use the command's records for commitments and money due. Do not extract record tables from prose pages or treat missing spending, training or investment history as zero. Report unavailable sources privately. Prepare a short dashboard, write weekly/<date> through pages.ts and update the huddle data through its connected storage. Scheduled preparation may read connected sources and write private pages. Sending email requires an explicit current authorization and recipients read from private settings. Never embed recipients or household names in this procedure.
